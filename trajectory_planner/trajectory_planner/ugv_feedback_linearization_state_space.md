# Feedback Linearization Formulation for Planar UGV Tracking ($\tilde{A}\chi + \tilde{B}\lambda$ State-Space Dynamics)

## 1. System Dynamics & State Representation

We consider a planar dynamic bicycle model for an Unmanned Ground Vehicle (UGV). The state vector $\chi(t)$ in global **inertial**? (Tactical Coverage 3.4.2 #69) coordinates is given by:

$$\chi(t) = \begin{bmatrix} x(t) \\ \dot{x}(t) \\ y(t) \\ \dot{y}(t) \end{bmatrix}$$

The physical control inputs to the system are:

$$u(t) = \begin{bmatrix} F_x(t) \\ \delta_f(t) \end{bmatrix}$$

where:
- $F_x(t)$ is the total longitudinal force produced along the vehicle body axis.
- $\delta_f(t)$ is the front-wheel steering angle relative to the chassis body axis.

---

## 2. Dynamic Equations of Motion with Full Non-Linear Slip Angles

Resolving the planar body dynamics into the inertial frame using the rotation matrix $R(\psi)$:

$$R(\psi) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\psi & -\sin\psi \\ \sin\psi & \cos\psi \end{bmatrix}$$

The inertial second-order position dynamics are derived as:

$$\begin{bmatrix} \ddot{x}(t) \\ \ddot{y}(t) \end{bmatrix} = R(\psi) \begin{bmatrix} a_x^B \\ a_y^B \end{bmatrix}$$

where the body-frame accelerations $a_x^B$ and $a_y^B$ incorporate centrifugal coupling:

$$\begin{aligned}
a_x^B &= \frac{1}{m} F_x + v_y \dot{\psi} \\
a_y^B &= \frac{1}{m} F_y^B - v_x \dot{\psi}
\end{aligned}$$

### Full Non-Linear Tire Sideslip Kinematics
We retain the full non-linear $\arctan$ formulation for tire sideslip angles with numerical regularization parameter $\varepsilon > 0$ to prevent division-by-zero singularities near zero longitudinal velocity:

$$\theta_{vf}(t) = \arctan\left( \frac{v_y + a\dot{\psi}}{\max(v_x, \varepsilon)} \right), \quad \theta_{vr}(t) = \arctan\left( \frac{v_y - b\dot{\psi}}{\max(v_x, \varepsilon)} \right)$$

The total body lateral force $F_y^B$ generated by the front and rear tires is:

$$F_y^B = 2 P_{C_f} \left( \delta_f - \theta_{vf} \right) - 2 P_{C_r} \theta_{vr}$$

Here:
- $m$ is the vehicle mass.
- $a, b$ are distances from the CG to the front and rear axles.
- $P_{C_f}, P_{C_r}$ are front and rear cornering stiffness parameters.
- $v_x, v_y$ are the body-frame velocity components:

$$\begin{bmatrix} v_x \\ v_y \end{bmatrix} = R^{-1}(\psi) \begin{bmatrix} \dot{x} \\ \dot{y} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \dot{x}\cos\psi + \dot{y}\sin\psi \\ -\dot{x}\sin\psi + \dot{y}\cos\psi \end{bmatrix}$$

---

## 3. Control-Affine Decomposition

The second-order output dynamics are written in control-affine form:

$$\begin{bmatrix} \ddot{x}(t) \\ \ddot{y}(t) \end{bmatrix} = f_{pos}(\chi, \psi, \dot{\psi}) + G_{pos}(\psi) \begin{bmatrix} F_x(t) \\ \delta_f(t) \end{bmatrix}$$

### Exact Non-Linear Drift Vector $f_{pos}(\cdot)$
$$f_{pos}(\cdot) = R(\psi) \begin{bmatrix} v_y \dot{\psi} \\ -\frac{2 P_{C_f} \arctan\left(\frac{v_y + a\dot{\psi}}{\max(v_x, \varepsilon)}\right) + 2 P_{C_r} \arctan\left(\frac{v_y - b\dot{\psi}}{\max(v_x, \varepsilon)}\right)}{m} - v_x \dot{\psi} \end{bmatrix}$$

### Decoupling Control Matrix $G_{pos}(\psi)$
$$G_{pos}(\psi) = R(\psi) \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{m} & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{2 P_{C_f}}{m} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\cos\psi}{m} & -\frac{2 P_{C_f} \sin\psi}{m} \\[6pt] \frac{\sin\psi}{m} & \frac{2 P_{C_f} \cos\psi}{m} \end{bmatrix}$$

### Inverse Decoupling Matrix $G_{pos}^{-1}(\psi)$
$$G_{pos}^{-1}(\psi) = \begin{bmatrix} m \cos\psi & m \sin\psi \\[6pt] -\frac{m \sin\psi}{2 P_{C_f}} & \frac{m \cos\psi}{2 P_{C_f}} \end{bmatrix}$$

---

## 4. Linearization into Controllable State-Space Form ($\tilde{A}\chi + \tilde{B}\lambda$)

We specify target controllable linear state dynamics in matrix form:

$$\dot{\chi}(t) = \tilde{A} \chi(t) + \tilde{B} \lambda(t)$$

where $\chi(t) = [x(t), \dot{x}(t), y(t), \dot{y}(t)]^T \in \mathbb{R}^4$, external virtual control input $\lambda(t) = [\lambda_x(t), \lambda_y(t)]^T \in \mathbb{R}^2$, and:

$$\tilde{A} = \text{Blockdiag}(\tilde{A}_x, \tilde{A}_y), \quad \tilde{B} = \text{Blockdiag}(\tilde{B}_x, \tilde{B}_y)$$

with sub-blocks chosen to be controllable (e.g., controllable canonical form):

$$\tilde{A}_x = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ \tilde{A}_{x,0} & \tilde{A}_{x,1} \end{bmatrix}, \quad \tilde{B}_x = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \tilde{B}_{x,1} \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\tilde{A}_y = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ \tilde{A}_{y,0} & \tilde{A}_{y,1} \end{bmatrix}, \quad \tilde{B}_y = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \tilde{B}_{y,1} \end{bmatrix}$$

The target linear accelerations $[\ddot{x}_{lin}, \ddot{y}_{lin}]^T$ dictated by the linear state-space structure are:

$$\begin{bmatrix} \ddot{x}_{lin} \\ \ddot{y}_{lin} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \tilde{A}_{x,0} x(t) + \tilde{A}_{x,1} \dot{x}(t) + \tilde{B}_{x,1} \lambda_x(t) \\ \tilde{A}_{y,0} y(t) + \tilde{A}_{y,1} \dot{y}(t) + \tilde{B}_{y,1} \lambda_y(t) \end{bmatrix}$$

---

## 5. Feedback Linearized Control Law

Equating the physical system accelerations to the linear state-space target accelerations:

$$f_{pos}(\cdot) + G_{pos}(\psi) \begin{bmatrix} F_x(t) \\ \delta_f(t) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \tilde{A}_{x,0} x(t) + \tilde{A}_{x,1} \dot{x}(t) + \tilde{B}_{x,1} \lambda_x(t) \\ \tilde{A}_{y,0} y(t) + \tilde{A}_{y,1} \dot{y}(t) + \tilde{B}_{y,1} \lambda_y(t) \end{bmatrix}$$

Solving explicitly for physical control inputs $[F_x(t), \delta_f(t)]^T$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} F_x(t) \\ \delta_f(t) \end{bmatrix} = G_{pos}^{-1}(\psi) \left( \begin{bmatrix} \tilde{A}_{x,0} x(t) + \tilde{A}_{x,1} \dot{x}(t) + \tilde{B}_{x,1} \lambda_x(t) \\ \tilde{A}_{y,0} y(t) + \tilde{A}_{y,1} \dot{y}(t) + \tilde{B}_{y,1} \lambda_y(t) \end{bmatrix} - f_{pos}(\cdot) \right)$$

### Explicit Scalar Control Solutions

Expanding the matrix inversion yields:

$$\begin{aligned}
F_x(t) &= m \cos\psi \left( \tilde{A}_{x,0} x(t) + \tilde{A}_{x,1} \dot{x}(t) + \tilde{B}_{x,1} \lambda_x(t) \right) \\
&\quad + m \sin\psi \left( \tilde{A}_{y,0} y(t) + \tilde{A}_{y,1} \dot{y}(t) + \tilde{B}_{y,1} \lambda_y(t) \right) - m v_y \dot{\psi} \\[12pt]
\delta_f(t) &= \frac{m}{2 P_{C_f}} \left[ -\sin\psi \left( \tilde{A}_{x,0} x(t) + \tilde{A}_{x,1} \dot{x}(t) + \tilde{B}_{x,1} \lambda_x(t) \right) + \cos\psi \left( \tilde{A}_{y,0} y(t) + \tilde{A}_{y,1} \dot{y}(t) + \tilde{B}_{y,1} \lambda_y(t) \right) + v_x \dot{\psi} \right] \\
&\quad + \arctan\left( \frac{v_y + a\dot{\psi}}{\max(v_x, \varepsilon)} \right) + \frac{P_{C_r}}{P_{C_f}} \arctan\left( \frac{v_y - b\dot{\psi}}{\max(v_x, \varepsilon)} \right)
\end{aligned}$$

---

## 6. Discrete-Time Zero-Order Hold (ZOH) Formulation

The continuous-time linear decoupled system $\dot{\chi}(t) = \tilde{A}\chi(t) + \tilde{B}\lambda_k(t)$ is discretized across sampling period $\Delta T$:

$$\chi_{k+1} = A_{discrete} \chi_k + B_{discrete} \lambda_k$$

where:

$$A_{discrete} = e^{\tilde{A} \Delta T}, \quad B_{discrete} = \int_{0}^{\Delta T} e^{\tilde{A}\tau} d\tau \tilde{B}$$

This discrete linear dynamic system can be directly plugged into downstream LTI linear MPC controllers (such as `acados` or `ACADO`).
